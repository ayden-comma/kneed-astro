Tivoli Road opened quietly in Windsor in 2013. Michael and Kerrie James built the bakery around a single idea: that good bread is a daily practice, not a product.

The country sourdough — their flagship — is made with a 14-year-old starter and a blend of stone-milled Victorian flours sourced from Wholegrain Milling in Gunnedah. It ferments slowly overnight, shaped by hand and baked in a deck oven until the crust reaches the precise shade of dark amber that Michael describes as "just before it burns".

The result is a loaf with extraordinary shelf life. A Tivoli Road country sourdough bought on Saturday is still excellent on Wednesday — better, in fact, once the crumb has had time to settle.
