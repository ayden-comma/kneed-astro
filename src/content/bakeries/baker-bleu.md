Baker Bleu is the bakery Mike and Meredith Russell built from scratch — a neighbourhood spot in Armadale that has become one of Melbourne's most respected sourdough destinations. Their bread is made with Australian-grown, stone-milled flour and fermented slowly for depth of flavour.

Mike Russell started Baker Bleu after stints at Baker D. Chirico, Iggy's in Sydney and Bourke Street Bakery. The loaf ferments for 48 hours, then bakes to a caramel-dark crust that shatters on the knife.

Baker Bleu now supplies some of Melbourne's best restaurants, including Attica and Gimlet. But the Armadale flagship still has locals ducking in before work to collect their daily loaf.
