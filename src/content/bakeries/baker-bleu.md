Baker Bleu is the bakery Mike and Meredith Russell built from scratch — a neighbourhood spot in Armadale that has become one of Melbourne's most respected sourdough destinations. Their bread is made with Australian-grown, stone-milled flour and fermented slowly for depth of flavour.
