Lune Croissanterie is widely regarded as one of the best croissant makers in the world. Founded by Kate Reid, a former aerospace engineer, Lune applies scientific precision to the art of laminated dough.

The result is a croissant with hundreds of paper-thin layers and a shattering, buttery crust. The interior is all honeycomb — open, airy, faintly sweet. It is made the same way every day, to tolerances most bakers don't bother with.
