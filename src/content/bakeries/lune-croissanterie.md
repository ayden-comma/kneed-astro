Lune Croissanterie is widely regarded as one of the best croissant makers in the world. Founded by Kate Reid, a former aerospace engineer, Lune applies scientific precision to the art of laminated dough — the result is a croissant with hundreds of paper-thin layers and a shattering, buttery crust.
