## Colour
Core: Ink #0E0C0A · Cream #F2EDE6 · Amber #C8833A
Warm neutrals: Paper light #FAF7F2 (inputs/raised) · Cream deep #E4DDD2 (cards/alt rows) · Border #C4BAAE (hairlines) · Grey #8A8178 (disabled/hints) · Slate #57504A (secondary text) · Graphite #2B2724 (body)
Amber states: Wash #F4E6D3 (highlight bg) · Light #E0A862 (hover/icons) · Amber #C8833A (accent) · Deep #A96C27 (pressed/links)
Secondary (optional, film-grade teal): Petrol #244A46 / Petrol light #35635D — one dark section or map markers ONLY
Functional: Error #A5432F (form errors only — red never decorative); focus ring = amber. Success = amber or a check, never green fill.
Collateral only (NOT website UI): Green #5F7350 · Plum #5C3A4E · Plum light #7D5468. No yellow ever (collides with amber).

## Type
Display: Josefin Sans 300/400, uppercase, tracking +0.10–0.14em (300 large, 400 smaller)
Labels/utility: Archivo Narrow 500/600, uppercase, +0.16–0.32em (eyebrows, meta, buttons, captions, nav)
Body: Barlow 400, sentence case, 1.7 line-height, Graphite on cream
Scale: Hero Josefin 300 58px +0.12em · H1 300 44px +0.14em · H2 400 30px +0.12em · H3 500 20px +0.10em · Eyebrow Archivo 500 12px +0.32em · Body Barlow 400 17px/1.7 · Small 14px/1.6 slate

## Logo
Wordmark ink-on-cream / cream-on-ink only. Clear space = height of "(K)". <~120px wide use small-size asset. Never recolour, no shadows/glows/gradients, never on mid-tone/busy backgrounds, never rebuild in live text.
Marks: icon (favicon/app), circle lockup (IG/TikTok/YT), square lockup (OG/share/FB).

## Voice (canonical for ALL copy)
Positioning: "A cultural and culinary exploration of bread and pastry, told through the bakers who live it."
Anchor: "(K)NEED isn't a platform you sign up to. It's a place you come back to."
Principles:
1. Pull up a chair — joining is an invitation, not a transaction. "We" to "you", host at the table, never system-to-user.
2. Point at the bread — words on films/bakers/loaves; never features, dashboards, functionality.
3. Something's in the oven — present tense, forward-leaning; there's always a next film. You're part of (K)Need, not a user of it.
Swaps (instead of → say):
- Create an account → Join (K)NEED
- Your (K)NEED account → Your password on (K)NEED
- Sign up for the newsletter → Get each film first
- Bakery submission form → Suggest a bakery
- Watch our content → Watch the film
- Manage account settings → Your details
Voice bends: on security/error/legal/accessibility surfaces, clarity first — plain expected words (password reset, failed login). Warm framing only where it adds clarity.
Micro examples: CTA "Join (K)NEED" · Confirmation "You've pulled up a chair. Welcome in." · Error "That email doesn't look right. Check it and try again."
